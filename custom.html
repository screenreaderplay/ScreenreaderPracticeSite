            Questions before you fly

            Can I take a pet dragon in the cabin?

            Dragons under 8 kg can travel in the cabin in a fireproof carrier. Larger dragons travel in the hold, which is heated to their liking.

            How much luggage is included?

            Every fare includes one cabin bag up to 10 kg. You can add a 23 kg checked bag when you book, for £35 each way.

            Can I change my dates after booking?

            Yes. Changes cost £25 per passenger, plus any difference in fare. Changes close 3 hours before departure.

            What happens if Atlantis sinks before my flight?

            We rebook you to Lemuria or Hy-Brasil at no extra cost, or give you a full refund.

            Is there step-free access at Mount Olympus airport?

            Yes. Every Wyvernway gate has step-free boarding. You can book assistance up to 48 hours before you fly, including help for guide dog owners.

            Wyvernway is a fictional company made for screen reader practice. No real flights are sold.
