Wyvernway practice site

Wyvernway is an imaginary flight comparison site that sells flights to mythical places. The same booking journey is built twice: once accessibly and once badly, so you can hear how the build changes the experience. Nothing here is real, and no data leaves the page.

Choose a version

  • Version 1

    Accessible

    Built from custom widgets that follow the ARIA Authoring Practices, plus plain patterns where they work better: type-to-search From and To boxes with a list of results, a cabin class combobox, a plain two-month calendar made of real buttons that read out the day, month and price, spinbuttons, tabs, switches, a slider, a tooltip, a carousel and modal dialogs. Use this one to compare VoiceOver on Mac with JAWS on Windows.

    Accessible
  • Version 2

    Inaccessible

    Looks and works exactly like version 1, but has a handful of realistic faults of the kind you find on real sites: missing states, an unlabelled button, results you can't reach, a calendar that loses your focus, a cookie message at the end of the page and a chatty carousel. Can you find them all?

    Deliberately inaccessible

Practice tasks

Try the same tasks on both versions, in order. For each step, note how you found the control, what the screen reader said, and how you knew it worked.

  1. Read the page title, then find the main heading and list the landmarks.
  2. Find the cookie message. Open the cookie settings, turn on Analytics cookies only, and save. How were you told it worked, and where did your focus go?
  3. Change the trip type to One way, then back to Return.
  4. Search for flights from Avalon to Xanadu by typing part of each name, then choosing from the results.
  5. Open the calendar from Travel dates. Go to next month, use the Cheapest button to find the cheapest day, then choose it. Then, still in the calendar, choose a return date a week later. When the calendar closes, where are you, and do you know what to do next?
  6. Add one child and change the cabin class to Business.
  7. Find out what "Include nearby airports" means, using the help button next to it.
  8. Turn on Direct flights only and Flexible dates, then search.
  9. Try to search with the same place in From and To. How are you told about the error?
  10. Sort the results by Cheapest. How do you know the list changed?
  11. Filter to flights that depart before 12:00.
  12. Open the flight details for the first result.
  13. Save the first flight to your trips. How do you know it is saved?
  14. Select a flight. Leave the email empty and try to confirm. Then fix it, add a checked bag, accept the terms and confirm.
  15. Find your booking reference, close the booking and check where your focus lands.
  16. In Popular destinations, find the price of flights to Valhalla, then use that slide to set Valhalla as your destination. Did anything move before you were ready?
  17. Open the answer to "What happens if Atlantis sinks before my flight?"
  18. Find out whether there is step-free access at Mount Olympus airport.

What to listen for: VoiceOver and JAWS

These are the places in version 1 where screen readers most often differ. Note what each one says and does, rather than assuming either is correct.

Modes and keys

  • When does JAWS switch to forms mode on its own, and when do you need Enter? Try the combobox, date grid, tabs, slider and spinbuttons.
  • With VoiceOver, do the arrow keys reach the widget, or do you need to interact with it first?

Comboboxes

  • From and To: after typing, is the number of results read out? Can you reach the results with Down arrow or VO+Right arrow, and are you back in the box after choosing?
  • Cabin class: is it announced as a combobox, a pop-up button or something else?

Date picker

  • When the calendar opens, do you hear "Choose your departure date", and is focus on a day?
  • Moving through the days with your usual table and button commands, do you hear the day, the month, "low price", "average price" or "high price", and "select as departure date" or "select as return date"?
  • Does selecting a day choose that exact day?
  • After choosing your departure date, is "Now choose your return date" read, while you stay in the calendar?
  • Two months are shown at once. Can you jump between them by heading (H in JAWS, the Headings rotor or VO+Command+H in VoiceOver)?
  • Do Previous month, Next month and Cheapest tell you which months are showing or move you to the cheapest day?
  • When you choose your return date, does the calendar close, put you back on Travel dates and read your new dates?

States and values

  • Flexible dates: "switch, on" or something else?
  • Spinbuttons: is the new number read when it changes? Can you find the + and − buttons, which are not in the Tab order?
  • Slider: do you hear "Before 12:00" or just "12"?
  • Tabs: do you hear the position, such as "2 of 3", and "selected"?

Dialog and messages

  • Can the virtual cursor or VoiceOver cursor leave the booking dialog?
  • Is the results count read after you change a filter or sort?
  • Is the new total read when you add an extra?

Carousel

  • Is "carousel" and "slide, 2 of 5" announced, or just "group"?
  • Does the carousel stop rotating when your screen reader cursor or focus is inside it? VoiceOver's cursor does not always move keyboard focus.
  • When you press Next, is the new slide read out?

Tooltip

  • When you Tab to the help button, is the tooltip text read as a description?
  • Can you read the tooltip with the virtual cursor or VoiceOver cursor, or does it vanish first?

Cookie settings

  • Is Essential cookies read as "switch, on, dimmed" or "unavailable"?
  • Are the descriptions under each switch read?
  • After saving, is the new cookie message read, and where is your focus?

Answer key for version 2

Show the deliberate problems in the inaccessible version
  • Main navigation: "Flights" is underlined as the current page, but aria-current is missing, so screen readers don't say it is the current page. WCAG 1.3.1
  • Trip type: Return and One way are radio buttons, but neither is ever announced as selected. 4.1.2
  • The swap button between From and To has no name, so it is read as just "button". 4.1.2, 2.4.6
  • From and To: typing announces "2 results available", but the results are hidden from screen readers, Down arrow does nothing and they can only be chosen with a mouse. 2.1.1, 4.1.2
  • Calendar: it opens, but focus stays on Travel dates and nothing tells you it opened. It is not modal, so you have to go looking for it. 2.4.3
  • Calendar days only say the number and price, for example "30 £106". The month, weekday, low or high price and "select as departure date" are missing. Low and high prices are shown only by colour. 1.3.1, 1.4.1, 2.4.6
  • After you choose a departure date, focus is thrown to the top of the page with no message, while the calendar stays open waiting for a return date. 2.4.3, 3.2.2
  • The cookie message shows at the bottom of the screen straight away, but it is last in the page, so screen reader users don't hear about it until the end and have to go to the bottom to dismiss it. 1.3.2, 2.4.3
  • Popular destinations: images have no alt text, so file names may be read. 1.1.1
  • Popular destinations: slides are labelled only "Slide 1", "Slide 2" and so on. Rotation doesn't stop when you focus or hover on the carousel, and each change is announced, even while you are reading something else. 2.2.2, 4.1.3

Wyvernway is fictional. Flights, airlines and places are invented for screen reader practice.
